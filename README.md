# PDF26

A PDF reader and note-taking app for Android tablets and phones, built for writing with a stylus.

PDF26 is a modified version of [PdfCraft](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft) by the ArtCraft
team and community. It is not made or endorsed by the ArtCraft Team. It runs the full PdfCraft
interface natively on Android and adds a notes toolbar and stylus features.

## Features

- **Notes toolbar** across the top: undo and redo, select, pen, highlighter, eraser, text, lasso,
  colours and sizes, and add page (blank, lined, grid or dotted).
- **Pen** strokes are steadied as you write, keep their sharp corners, and are saved with only the
  points they need.
- **Highlighter**: freehand, straight (held level along a line of text) or over text (drag across
  the words).
- **Hold to snap**: hold the pen still at the end of a stroke and it becomes a clean line,
  rectangle, oval, triangle or polygon.
- **Lasso**: loop around writing to select it, then move, recolour, copy, paste or delete it.
- **Stylus**: with Stylus only on, only the pen writes and a finger scrolls. Palm rejection ignores
  a resting hand. The stylus side button or eraser end erases.
- **Gestures**: tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo; flick to scroll.
- Everything else PdfCraft does: forms, comments, organising pages, OCR, export and more.

## Install

Download `PDF26.apk` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest), open it on your device, and
allow installs from that source when Android asks. The release APK is a debug build for testing.
